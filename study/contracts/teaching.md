# Beginner teaching contract

## Before teaching

Read the target outcomes, full permitted source evidence and relevant prerequisite concepts. Check source/agent-channel policy first. Use precise physical-page/region or original-line citations, not a source title alone. Inspect original images before relying on extracted mathematics. Admit missing evidence; do not fill gaps with fabricated source claims.

Ask a short readiness question or offer a beginner explanation if prior knowledge is unknown. Teach the bridge needed now: arithmetic/signs, percentages, years/months, algebra, payoff versus profit or compounding conventions as relevant. Do not require a later unexplained concept to understand the current lesson.

## Lesson structure

1. Explain the motivation and observable learning outcomes.
2. Teach prerequisite foundations before using them.
3. Define every term and symbol, currency/unit, time basis, annualization/compounding convention and model assumption before applying a formula. Explain dimensionless rates/exponents and distinguish decimal from percentage notation.
4. Give intuition and step-by-step reasoning/derivations; do not merely expand slide bullets into a summary. Label supplemental explanations and the limits of any informal derivation.
5. Work an example completely: setup, convention, intermediate calculations, result, units, rounding and a reasonableness/boundary check. Independently verify all used formulas and answers; generated solutions are not official supplied solutions.
6. Explain common misconceptions and model limits. Describe referenced figures/tables accessibly with the original evidence available. Keep original source conflicts and labelled corrections visible.
7. Provide progressively scaffolded practice and a teach-back prompt matched to each outcome. Put answers at distinct explicit anchors after the practice, so the learner can attempt first. Label supplemental exercises/answers.
8. Check understanding with a learner response; diagnose reasoning, give focused feedback and retry a simpler step when needed. Distinguish a correct numerical answer from a justified explanation. State uncertainty honestly.

Preserve the learner's own notes/teach-back. Do not copy private learner content into canonical lessons, indexes, reviews, logs or fixtures. A complete authored lesson is prepared content, not evidence that a particular learner has learned it.

## Independent acceptance

Every production lesson must score 2 (adequate) on **each** of eight dimensions; do not average away a defect:

- Source fidelity and clear supplement/conflict/uncertainty labels.
- Beginner prerequisites before use.
- Symbols, units and assumptions defined.
- Intuition plus stepwise explanation.
- Correct, checkable worked examples.
- Observable outcomes, scaffolded exercises, separate answers and teach-back.
- Misconceptions/limits and faithful accessible figures/tables.
- Precise complete evidence citations.

A genuinely different reviewer checks every lesson, formula and used solution against sources and independent calculations; a random sample or self-authored approval is insufficient. Record a score and concrete source/lesson evidence for every dimension. Bind review inputs by exact hashes; changed inputs need fresh independent review. Learner success and final browser/release acceptance remain separate gates.
