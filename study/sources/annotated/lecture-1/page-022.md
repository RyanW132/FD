## Forward payoff table

**Source region:** upper scenario and central cash-flow table. **Classification:** substantive.

Suppose the delivery price is $K=105$.

| $S_T$ | Asset received | Payment | Long payoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| £80 | +80 | −105 | −25 |
| £105 | +105 | −105 | 0 |
| £130 | +130 | −105 | +25 |

Amounts in this example are pounds per unit. $S_T$ is the market value of the underlying at maturity $T$.

## Long payoff formula

**Source region:** lower general-result box, left equation. **Classification:** substantive.

At maturity, the long-forward payoff is

$$\Pi_T^{\mathrm{long}}=S_T-K.$$

$K$ is the agreed delivery price and $S_T$ the maturity market price; both use the same currency per underlying unit. $\Pi_T^{\mathrm{long}}$ is the maturity payoff per unit.

## Short payoff formula

**Source region:** lower general-result box, right equation. **Classification:** substantive.

At maturity, the short-forward payoff is

$$\Pi_T^{\mathrm{short}}=K-S_T.$$

$K$ is the delivery price, $S_T$ the maturity market price, and $\Pi_T^{\mathrm{short}}$ the maturity payoff per unit.
