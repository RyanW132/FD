## Choice versus obligation

**Source region:** upper two-contract box. **Classification:** substantive.

You may acquire an asset in three months for £100. Compare:
1. You are **obliged** to buy it for £100.
2. You have the **right, but not the obligation**, to buy it for £100.

## Option distinction

**Source region:** middle key-distinction box. **Classification:** substantive.

- A forward or futures contract creates an obligation.
- An option gives its buyer a right.
- The option buyer pays a premium for that right.
