## Forward zero sum

**Source region:** lower what-do-you-notice box. **Classification:** substantive.

$$\Pi_T^{\mathrm{long}}+\Pi_T^{\mathrm{short}}=0.$$

One party's gain is the other party's loss. The source compares the two maturity payoffs for the same $K$, $S_T$, and underlying unit.

**Authored source caution — not original slide content:** Both embedded diagrams label the vertical axis Profit whereas the equations describe maturity payoffs. Equality to profit requires explicit zero-initial-value/no-premium and no additional financing/transaction-cost assumptions; the original axes are not relabelled.
