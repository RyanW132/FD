## Call definition

**Source region:** upper call-option box. **Classification:** substantive.

A call gives its holder the right, but not the obligation, to buy an underlying asset for the strike price $K$.

## Call exercise question

**Source region:** middle exercise-decision box. **Classification:** substantive.

Suppose $K=100$. Would you exercise the call if the market price at maturity is $S_T=70$, $S_T=100$, or $S_T=130$?

## Call payoff formula

**Source region:** bottom call-payoff equation. **Classification:** substantive.

$$C_T=\max(S_T-K,0).$$

$C_T$ is the maturity call payoff per unit, $S_T$ the maturity underlying price and $K$ the strike in the same currency per unit. The maximum compares **the full difference** $S_T-K$ with zero.
