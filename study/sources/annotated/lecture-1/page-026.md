## Long forward slope

**Source region:** right reading-the-graph box. **Classification:** substantive.

The source says the payoff is

$$\Pi_T^{\mathrm{long}}=S_T-K.$$

The line has slope **+1**: a £1 increase in $S_T$ increases the payoff by £1 per unit. $K$ is the delivery price and $S_T$ the underlying price at maturity.
