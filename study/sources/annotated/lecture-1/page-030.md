## Margin balance question

**Source region:** middle calculation box. **Classification:** substantive.

A trader deposits initial margin of £2,000. The futures position loses £600 on Day 1 and £500 on Day 2. What is the margin balance after Day 2?

![Original full-page evidence for lecture-1-p030-margin-balance-question; physical page 30](../../assets/lecture-1/page-030.png)

<a id="lecture-1-p030-margin-balance-answer"></a>

## Margin balance answer

**Source region:** lower answer box. **Classification:** substantive.

$$2{,}000-600-500=900.$$

If the maintenance margin exceeds £900, the trader receives a margin call. The slide supplies no numerical maintenance-margin threshold beyond this condition.

![Original full-page evidence for lecture-1-p030-margin-balance-answer; physical page 30](../../assets/lecture-1/page-030.png)
