## Put definition

**Source region:** upper put-option box. **Classification:** substantive.

A put gives its holder the right, but not the obligation, to sell an underlying asset for the strike price $K$.

## Put payoff formula

**Source region:** bottom put-payoff equation. **Classification:** substantive.

$$P_T=\max(K-S_T,0).$$

$P_T$ is the maturity put payoff per unit; $K$ is the strike and $S_T$ the underlying price, in matching currency per unit. The full difference **$K-S_T$**, not $S_T-K$, is compared with zero.
