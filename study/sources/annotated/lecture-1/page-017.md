## Chart interest rate

**Source region:** embedded Graph A1, left interest-rate derivatives panel. **Classification:** substantive.

**Accessible figure transcription, not digitised observations.** Embedded title: “OTC trading of interest rate and FX derivatives outpaced exchange trading”. Subtitle: “Daily average turnover, in trillions of US dollars”. Graph A1.

Left panel: **Interest rate derivatives**. Horizontal calendar ticks: 01, 04, 07, 10, 13, 16, 19. Vertical ticks on the panel's right: 0, 3, 6, 9, 12 (trillions of US dollars). Red solid jagged series: **XTD** (exchange-traded derivatives); blue circular survey observations with dotted connecting lines: **OTC** (over-the-counter derivatives).

The red series fluctuates considerably and ends above 12; the blue observations rise from near zero in 2001 to about 6 in the final survey. These are visual approximations only: this panel shows daily-average turnover, not exposure or outstanding risk. No exact point values beyond the displayed ticks are invented.

## Chart fx

**Source region:** embedded Graph A1, right foreign-exchange derivatives panel. **Classification:** substantive.

Right panel: **Foreign exchange derivatives**. Horizontal ticks: 01, 04, 07, 10, 13, 16, 19. **Different vertical axes**: red **XTD (lhs)** uses left ticks 0.0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4; blue **OTC (rhs)** uses right ticks 0, 1, 2, 3, 4. Units for both are daily-average turnover in trillions of US dollars.

Blue circles and dotted survey-date interpolation increase approximately from below 1 to above 4; red continuous monthly observations fluctuate on the much smaller left-hand scale. The two series cannot be compared by plotted height without their respective axes. Red's endpoint is around 0.1–0.2 on the left axis; blue's endpoint is over 4 on the right. These are approximate visual descriptions, not extracted numerical data. The slide title's claim is that most FX derivative trading occurs OTC.

## Chart provenance

**Source region:** embedded graph legend, footnotes, sources and lower source caption. **Classification:** substantive.

Legend and footnotes:
- XTD = exchange-traded derivatives; OTC = over-the-counter derivatives.
- Footnote 1: turnover on exchanges worldwide, at monthly frequency.
- Footnote 2: turnover in April, adjusted for local and cross-border inter-dealer double-counting. The dashed line shows a linear interpolation of data between Triennial Surveys.
- Embedded sources: Euromoney TRADEDATA; Futures Industry Association; The Options Clearing Corporation; BIS derivatives statistics. © Bank for International Settlements.
- Slide caption: BIS, Triennial Central Bank Survey, 2019. OTC observations occur at survey dates; dotted lines interpolate between them.

Dashed/dotted connecting segments are interpolation, not additional measured OTC observations. This is a faithful description of the supplied historical chart, not a claim about current market volumes.
