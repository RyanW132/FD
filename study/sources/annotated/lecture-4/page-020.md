## Offsetting forward portfolio

Construct a portfolio at time $t$:
1. Keep the existing long forward with delivery price $K$.
2. Enter a new short forward with delivery price $F_{t,T}$. A new fairly priced forward has zero value.

At delivery, the combined payoff is certain:
$$(S_T-K)+(F_{t,T}-S_T)=F_{t,T}-K.$$

## Discounted forward value

Discounting this certain payment gives
$$f_t=(F_{t,T}-K)e^{-r(T-t)}.$$

## No income value

For a no-income asset, substituting $F_{t,T}=S_te^{r(T-t)}$ gives the equivalent expression
$$f_t=S_t-Ke^{-r(T-t)}.$$
