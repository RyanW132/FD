## Risk transfer uses

Forward and futures contracts allow market participants to transfer price risk across time.
- A hedger can lock in a future purchase or sale price.
- A speculator can take a view on a future price without initially purchasing the underlying asset.
- An arbitrageur can exploit inconsistent spot and derivative prices.
- Observable futures prices can contribute to price discovery.

## Futures not forecast

A futures price reflects today's market-clearing price for future delivery. It is not necessarily an unbiased forecast of the future spot price.
