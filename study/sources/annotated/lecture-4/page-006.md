## Short sale cashflow table

An investor trades 500 shares initially priced at $120. The share pays a $1 dividend and is worth $100 when the position is closed.

| Cash flow | Buy shares | Short shares |
|---|---:|---:|
| Initial share transaction | −$60,000 | +$60,000 |
| Dividend | +$500 | −$500 |
| Closing share transaction | +$50,000 | −$50,000 |
| Net profit | −$9,500 | +$9,500 |
