## Income overpriced strategy

The fair forward price is $886.60. If quoted at $910: borrow to buy the bond and short the forward. Use the coupon to service the corresponding borrowing. At maturity, deliver the bond for $910 and repay $886.60. Profit: $23.40.

## Income underpriced strategy

If quoted at $870: short the bond and invest the proceeds after setting aside the present value of the coupon compensation. Go long the forward at $870. At maturity, the investment provides $886.60. Profit: $16.60.
