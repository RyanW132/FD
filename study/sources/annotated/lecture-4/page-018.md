## Known yield formula

If the asset provides a continuously compounded income yield $q$, the no-arbitrage forward price is
$$F_{0,T}=S_0e^{(r-q)T}.$$

## Known yield example

Let $S_0=25$, $r=10\%$, $q=3.96\%$, and $T=0.5$. Then
$$F_{0,T}=25e^{(0.10-0.0396)\times0.5}=\$25.77.$$

## Cash versus yield

Use $(S_0-I)e^{rT}$ for known cash amounts and $S_0e^{(r-q)T}$ for income proportional to the asset's value.
