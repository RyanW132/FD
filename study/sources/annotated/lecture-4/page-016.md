## Bond income data pv

A bond is priced at $900. It will pay a $40 coupon after four months. A nine-month forward is considered. The four-month zero rate is 3%, and the nine-month zero rate is 4%, both continuously compounded. The coupon's present value is
$$I=40e^{-0.03(4/12)}=\$39.60.$$
