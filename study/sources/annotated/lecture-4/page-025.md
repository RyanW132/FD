## Usd aud forward example

Suppose $S_0=0.9800$ USD per AUD, $r_{\mathrm{USD}}=1\%$, $r_{\mathrm{AUD}}=3\%$, $T=2$. The no-arbitrage forward rate is
$$F_{0,T}=0.9800e^{(0.01-0.03)\times2}=0.9416\ \text{USD per AUD}.$$
