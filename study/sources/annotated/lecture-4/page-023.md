## Index pricing

A stock index can be treated as an investment asset paying a dividend yield $q$. Under the standard assumptions,
$$F_{0,T}=S_0e^{(r-q)T}.$$

## Index example

For a three-month contract, let $S_0=1{,}300$, $r=5\%$, $q=1\%$, $T=0.25$. Then
$$F_{0,T}=1{,}300e^{(0.05-0.01)\times0.25}=1{,}313.07.$$
