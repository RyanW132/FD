## Daily gain formula

For $N$ long contracts, each covering $Q$ units, the daily gain is
$$\Delta V_t=NQ(F_t-F_{t-1}).$$

## Margin mechanics

Initial margin is deposited when the position is opened. Daily gains and losses enter the margin account through variation margin. If the balance falls below the maintenance margin, a margin call may require the account to be replenished.

## Daily loss example

Two long contracts cover 100 units each. If the futures price falls from $52 to $50.50,
$$\Delta V=2\times100\times(50.50-52)=-\$300.$$
