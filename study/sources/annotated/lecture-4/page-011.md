## Cash and carry replication

Consider an investment asset that pays no income before delivery.

At time 0:
1. Borrow $S_0$.
2. Buy one unit of the asset.
3. Short a forward contract for delivery at $T$.

At $T$, deliver the asset for $F_{0,T}$ and repay $S_0e^{rT}$.

## No income price

No arbitrage requires
$$F_{0,T}=S_0e^{rT}.$$
This is a cost-of-carry result, not a forecast of $S_T$.
