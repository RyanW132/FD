## Currency arbitrage steps

If the two-year forward rate is 0.9300 USD per AUD:
1. Borrow 1,000 AUD and convert it to 980 USD.
2. Invest 980 USD at 1% for two years, producing $980e^{0.01\times2}=\$999.80$.
3. The AUD loan grows to $1{,}000e^{0.03\times2}=1{,}061.84$ AUD.
4. Buy 1,061.84 AUD forward for $1{,}061.84\times0.9300=\$987.51$.
5. Use the AUD to repay the loan.
