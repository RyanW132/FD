## Asset distinction

For an investment asset, cash-and-carry arbitrage often enforces a precise relationship between spot and forward prices. For a consumption asset, inventory may provide operational benefits, so the relationship may involve a convenience yield and need not be enforced by the same arbitrage strategy.
