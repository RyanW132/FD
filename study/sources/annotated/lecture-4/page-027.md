## Commodity carry components

Physical commodities may involve financing cost $r$; storage and insurance costs $u$; and convenience yield $y$, the non-cash benefit of holding inventory.

## Commodity proportional formula

If proportional carry components are used,
$$F_{0,T}=S_0e^{(r+u-y)T}.$$

## Commodity cash storage formula

If storage costs are known cash amounts with present value $U$, and there is no convenience yield,
$$F_{0,T}=(S_0+U)e^{rT}.$$

## Consumption inventory benefit

Convenience yield can make holding physical inventory more valuable than holding only a forward contract. Reverse cash-and-carry arbitrage may therefore be unavailable.
