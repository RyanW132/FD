## Deterministic rates

Forward and futures prices are equal when interest rates are deterministic and the contracts otherwise have the same underlying and delivery date.

## Uncertain rate effects

When rates are uncertain, daily settlement can create a difference:
- If futures gains tend to occur when interest rates are high, those gains can be reinvested at favourable rates, tending to make the futures price exceed the forward price.
- If futures gains tend to occur when rates are low, the opposite tendency arises.

## Variation margin mechanism

The difference comes from the timing and reinvestment of daily variation-margin cash flows, not from a different terminal payoff formula.
