## Forward upper bound

Cash-and-carry can still rule out a forward price that is too high:
$$F_{0,T}\le S_0e^{rT}$$
under the basic no-income assumptions. Ruling out a forward price that is too low requires the reverse cash-and-carry strategy, which normally involves shorting the asset.

## Restricted short consequence

If investors cannot short the asset and do not already own it, the lower-bound arbitrage may be unavailable. The equality $F_{0,T}=S_0e^{rT}$ is therefore not guaranteed by that argument alone.

## Existing owner substitution

For an investment asset held by many investors, existing owners may still sell the asset and buy it forward, helping enforce the relationship.
