## Equivalent accumulation

Equivalent rates generate the same accumulation over the same period:
$$e^r=\left(1+\frac{R_m}{m}\right)^m.$$

## Conversion formulas

Hence,
$$R_m=m\left(e^{r/m}-1\right),\qquad r=m\ln\left(1+\frac{R_m}{m}\right).$$

## Semiannual example

A nominal rate of 10% with semiannual compounding is equivalent to
$$r=2\ln(1.05)=0.09758,$$
or 9.758% with continuous compounding.
