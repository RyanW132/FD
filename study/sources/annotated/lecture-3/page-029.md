## Fra notation

Define:
- $L$: notional principal;
- $R_K$: fixed rate agreed in the FRA;
- $R_M$: market reference rate observed at $T_1$;
- $\delta=T_2-T_1$: year fraction for the underlying period.

## Fra terminal difference

For the party that receives fixed and pays floating, the cash flow at $T_2$ is
$$L(R_K-R_M)\delta.$$

## Fra start settlement

If settlement occurs at $T_1$, its value is
$$\frac{L(R_K-R_M)\delta}{1+R_M\delta}.$$
The opposite party receives the negative of this amount. **Notation clarification — not original slide text:** the denominator discounts the entire interest difference; the slide uses a simple period-rate settlement convention, not the continuous discount-factor formula.
