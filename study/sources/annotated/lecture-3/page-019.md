## Bootstrap procedure

Zero rates can be inferred from prices of traded instruments.
- A zero-coupon instrument reveals its maturity's discount factor directly.
- A coupon bond contains several cash flows.
- Once earlier discount factors are known, subtract the present value of the earlier coupons and solve for the next discount factor.
- Repeat sequentially along the maturity structure.
