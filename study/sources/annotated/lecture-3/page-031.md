## Fra exposure questions

For each exposure, decide whether the firm should pay fixed or receive fixed.
1. A company will borrow £10 million in six months and fears that rates will rise.
2. An investor will lend £10 million in six months and fears that rates will fall.

## Fra exposure answers

1. The future borrower should pay fixed and receive floating. If rates rise, the FRA gain offsets the higher borrowing cost.
2. The future lender should receive fixed and pay floating. If rates fall, the FRA gain offsets the lower lending income.
