## Coupon data

## Coupon pricing calculation

Assuming continuous compounding,
$$B=3e^{-0.05\times0.5}+3e^{-0.058\times1}+3e^{-0.064\times1.5}+103e^{-0.068\times2}=\$98.39.$$
