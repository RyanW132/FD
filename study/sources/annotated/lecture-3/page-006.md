## Quotation convention

The quotation is incomplete unless its compounding convention is stated. The quoted rate alone is therefore not enough to determine the terminal value.

## One year example

Invest $100 at a nominal annual rate of 10%.
- Annual compounding: $100\times1.10=\$110.00$.
- Semiannual compounding: $100(1+0.10/2)^2=\$110.25$.
- Continuous compounding: $100e^{0.10}=\$110.52$.
