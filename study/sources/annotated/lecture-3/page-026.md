## One two forward example

Suppose the continuously compounded zero rates are 3% for one year and 4% for two years. The one-year rate beginning one year from today is
$$R_F(1,2)=\frac{0.04\times2-0.03\times1}{2-1}=5\%.$$

## Strategy terminal values

Both investment strategies therefore produce the same terminal value:
$$100e^{0.03\times1}e^{0.05\times1}=100e^{0.04\times2}=\$108.33.$$

## Forward not forecast

The 5% forward rate is implied by today's curve. It is not a guarantee that the one-year market rate observed in one year's time will equal 5%.
