## Zero rate

The $T$-year zero rate is the return on an investment that makes no intermediate payments and pays only at time $T$.

## Discount factor

With a continuously compounded zero rate $R(T)$, the discount factor is
$$P(0,T)=e^{-R(T)T}.$$

## Cashflow present value

The present value of a cash flow $C_T$ is
$$PV(C_T)=C_TP(0,T)=C_Te^{-R(T)T}.$$

## Five year example

If the five-year zero rate is 5%, then
$$100e^{0.05\times5}=\$128.40,\qquad100e^{-0.05\times5}=\$77.88.$$
