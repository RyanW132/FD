## Duration small change

Suppose the yield rises by 10 basis points: $\Delta y=+0.001$.

Duration predicts
$$\frac{\Delta B}{B}\approx-2.653\times0.001=-0.002653,$$
so
$$\Delta B\approx-94.213\times2.653\times0.001=-0.250.$$

## Duration new price

The estimated new price is $94.213-0.250=93.963$.

Direct repricing at $y=12.1\%$ gives approximately 93.963, so duration is very accurate for this small change.
