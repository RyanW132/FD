## Par discount factors

For the two-year zero curve,
$$d=e^{-0.068\times2}=0.87284,$$
and
$$A=e^{-0.05\times0.5}+e^{-0.058\times1}+e^{-0.064\times1.5}+e^{-0.068\times2}=3.70027.$$

## Par calculation

With two coupons per year,
$$c=\frac{100\times2\times(1-0.87284)}{3.70027}=6.87.$$
The two-year par yield is approximately 6.87% per annum with semiannual coupon payments.
