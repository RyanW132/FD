## Yield definition

The yield to maturity $y$ is the single discount rate that reproduces the observed bond price:
$$B=\sum_{i=1}^{n}C_i e^{-yt_i}.$$

## Yield versus zero

The zero curve contains one rate for each maturity. Yield to maturity compresses all of the bond's cash flows and the observed price into one internal rate of return.
