## Fra definition mechanics

A forward rate agreement (FRA) is an over-the-counter contract that fixes the interest rate applied to a notional principal during a future period $[T_1,T_2]$.
- The principal is normally not exchanged.
- The parties exchange only an interest-rate difference.
- One party receives fixed and pays the future market reference rate.
- The other party pays fixed and receives the market rate.
- Settlement normally occurs at $T_1$, so the $T_2$ difference is discounted back to $T_1$.

## Fra purpose

A borrower can use an FRA to protect against rising rates; a lender can use one to protect against falling rates.
