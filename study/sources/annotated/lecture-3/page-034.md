## Duration first order

Because $dB/dy=-BD$, a small yield change $\Delta y$ produces
$$\frac{\Delta B}{B}\approx-D\Delta y.$$

## Basis point

One basis point is $1\ \mathrm{bp}=0.01\%=0.0001$. A 10-basis-point increase means $\Delta y=+0.001$, not 0.1.
