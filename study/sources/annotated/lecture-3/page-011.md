## Growth questions

An investment grows from $5,000 to $5,800 in 18 months.
1. What is the continuously compounded annual rate?
2. What nominal annual rate with semiannual compounding is equivalent?

**Source-error correction — authored, not original slide text:** for growth from $5,000 to $5,800 over $1.5$ years, the correct continuous annual rate is $r=\ln(5800/5000)/1.5=9.894667007884882\%$. The equivalent nominal annual semiannual rate is $R_2=2(e^{r/2}-1)=10.14351489971605\%$ when $r$ is used as a decimal. The distinct effective annual rate is $e^r-1=10.400742136017955\%$. The source’s printed rates of 10.15% and 10.41% are not correct answers.
