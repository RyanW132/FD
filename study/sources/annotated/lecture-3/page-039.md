## Duration convexity example

For a 100-basis-point yield increase, $\Delta y=0.01$. The duration-convexity estimate is
$$\frac{\Delta B}{B}\approx-2.653\times0.01+\frac12\times7.570\times(0.01)^2.$$
Therefore,
$$B_{\mathrm{estimated}}=94.213[1-0.02653+0.0003785]=91.749.$$

## Duration convexity comparison
