## Par yield definition

The par yield for maturity $T$ is the coupon rate that makes a bond's price equal to its face value.

## Annuity and principal factors

Let $m$ be the number of coupon payments per year, let
$$A=\sum_{i=1}^{n}P(0,t_i)$$
be the present value of receiving $1 on every coupon date, and let $d=P(0,T)$.

## Par equation

For face value $100,
$$A\frac{c}{m}+100d=100.$$
Therefore,
$$c=\frac{100m(1-d)}{A}.$$
Here $c$ is the annual coupon in dollars on $100 face value, numerically equal to the annual coupon rate in percent.
