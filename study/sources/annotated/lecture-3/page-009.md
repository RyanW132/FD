## Exponential limit

The exponential function is defined by
$$e^x=\lim_{m\to\infty}\left(1+\frac{x}{m}\right)^m.$$

## Accumulation limit

As the compounding frequency becomes arbitrarily large,
$$\lim_{m\to\infty}\left(1+\frac{r}{m}\right)^{mT}=e^{rT}.$$

## Continuous values

Therefore, under continuous compounding,
$$FV=Ae^{rT},\qquad PV=FV e^{-rT}.$$

## Sign check

Compounding moves money forward with $e^{rT}$; discounting moves it backward with $e^{-rT}$.
