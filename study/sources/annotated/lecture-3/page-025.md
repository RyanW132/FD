## Forward rate definition

A forward rate is the future interest rate implied today by current zero rates.

## Forward accumulation

If $R_1$ and $R_2$ are continuously compounded zero rates for maturities $T_1<T_2$, no arbitrage requires
$$e^{R_2T_2}=e^{R_1T_1}e^{R_F(T_2-T_1)}.$$

## Forward rate formula

Hence,
$$R_F(T_1,T_2)=\frac{R_2T_2-R_1T_1}{T_2-T_1}.$$

## Discount factor ratio

Equivalently, using discount factors,
$$e^{-R_F(T_2-T_1)}=\frac{P(0,T_2)}{P(0,T_1)}.$$
