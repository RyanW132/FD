## Future value

If $A$ is invested for $T$ years at a nominal annual rate $R_m$, compounded $m$ times per year, its future value is
$$FV=A\left(1+\frac{R_m}{m}\right)^{mT}.$$

## Present value

The corresponding present value is
$$PV=FV\left(1+\frac{R_m}{m}\right)^{-mT}.$$

## Quarterly example

At 8% per annum with quarterly compounding, the two-year value of $1,000 is
$$1{,}000\left(1+\frac{0.08}{4}\right)^8=\$1{,}171.66.$$
