The current futures price for a Treasury bond deliverable in 3 months is 101.50. The bond’s coupon is 6% per annum, with semiannual payments, and the next coupon is due in 4 months. The risk-free rate is 5% (continuous compounding).

- Explain qualitatively what factors might cause the current futures price (101.50) to be higher or lower than the current spot price of the bond.
- How might the timing of the next coupon payment affect the relationship between the spot price and the futures price?
- If the bond were zero-coupon, would you expect the futures price to be higher or lower than the spot price? Briefly explain.
