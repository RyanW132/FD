A manufacturing company uses large quantities of copper in its production process and is concerned about rising copper prices over the next six months.

- What derivative instrument could the company use to hedge this price risk?
- Would it take a long or short position?
- Identify one potential risk that remains even after hedging.

<a id="seminar-2-solutions-p002-q2-solution"></a>

## Supplied solution: copper hedge

**Physical page 2; source region: Supplied solution: copper hedge.**

![Original full uncropped physical page 2](../../assets/seminar-2-solutions/page-002.png)

[Preserved raw extraction](../../extracted/seminar-2-solutions/page-002.md#page-2)

The company could use a futures contract (or forward contract) on copper to hedge rising prices. It should take a long position in copper futures, locking in the purchase price for future delivery and protecting against potential increases.

The hedge may not be perfect because of basis risk: futures and actual purchase prices may not move identically. Other residual risks include maturity, quantity, or quality differences relative to actual copper needs.

A non-dividend-paying stock is currently priced at $50. The risk-free rate is 6% per annum, with continuous compounding.

- Calculate the 6-month forward price for the stock.
- If the time to maturity were instead 1 year, what would the forward price be?

<a id="seminar-2-solutions-p002-q3-solution"></a>

## Supplied solution: continuous-compounding forward prices

**Physical page 2; source region: Supplied solution: continuous-compounding forward prices.**

![Original full uncropped physical page 2](../../assets/seminar-2-solutions/page-002.png)

[Preserved raw extraction](../../extracted/seminar-2-solutions/page-002.md#page-2)

For a non-dividend-paying asset under continuous compounding,

$$F_0=S_0e^{rT},$$

where $S_0=50$, $r=0.06$, and maturity $T$ is in years.

- Six months ($T=0.5$): $F_0=50e^{0.06\times0.5}=50e^{0.03}=50\times1.03045=51.52$.
- One year ($T=1$): $F_0=50e^{0.06\times1}=50e^{0.06}=50\times1.06184=53.09$.

The supplied rounded forward prices are approximately $51.52 and $53.09 respectively.
