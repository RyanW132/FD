## Basis example inputs

**Source region:** upper producer scenario. **Classification:** substantive.

A producer shorts futures at $F_1=$52. When the hedge closes, $S_2=$47 and $F_2=$48.

![Original full-page evidence for lecture-2-p013-basis-example-inputs; physical page 13](../../assets/lecture-2/page-013.png)

<a id="lecture-2-p013-basis-example-cashflows"></a>

## Basis example cashflows

**Source region:** middle effective-selling-price calculation. **Classification:** substantive.

The cash-market sale produces $47. The short-futures gain is

$$F_1-F_2=52-48=\$4.$$

Hence effective selling price = 47 + 4 = **$51**.

![Original full-page evidence for lecture-2-p013-basis-example-cashflows; physical page 13](../../assets/lecture-2/page-013.png)

<a id="lecture-2-p013-basis-example-check"></a>

## Basis example check

**Source region:** lower equivalent basis calculation. **Classification:** substantive.

Equivalently,

$$F_1+b_2=52+(47-48)=\$51.$$

The closing basis $b_2=S_2-F_2$ is −$1 per unit; its minus sign is preserved.
