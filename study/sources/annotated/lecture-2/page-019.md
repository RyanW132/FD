## Airline cross hedge inputs

**Source region:** upper scenario and numerical inputs. **Classification:** substantive.

An airline expects to purchase **2 million gallons of jet fuel in one month**. There is no suitable jet-fuel futures contract, so it considers heating-oil futures.
- Exposure: $Q_A=2{,}000{,}000$ gallons.
- Heating-oil contract size: $Q_F=42{,}000$ gallons.
- $\sigma_S=0.0263$.
- $\sigma_F=0.0313$.
- $\rho=0.928$.

The standard deviations are for price changes as defined on page 17. Their numerical currency/time-estimation units are not separately specified on this slide; no observation frequency is invented.
