## Basis definition

**Source region:** upper basis equation. **Classification:** substantive.

The basis is

$$b_t=S_t-F_t.$$

$S_t$ is the spot price and $F_t$ the futures price at the same time $t$, in matching quotation units. Basis is **spot minus futures**.

## Closing basis

**Source region:** central first two bullets. **Classification:** substantive.

- The basis may strengthen or weaken while the hedge is open.
- The closing basis determines the effective hedged price.

## Delivery convergence

**Source region:** central convergence bullet. **Classification:** substantive.

For the **same underlying and delivery terms**, spot and futures prices converge at delivery, so $b_T=0$.

![Original full-page evidence for lecture-2-p014-delivery-convergence; physical page 14](../../assets/lecture-2/page-014.png)

## Cross hedge basis

**Source region:** lower cross-hedge bullet. **Classification:** substantive.

Cross hedges generally retain basis risk because the exposure and futures contract reference different assets.
