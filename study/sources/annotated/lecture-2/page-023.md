## Market regression

**Source region:** upper explanatory sentence and excess-return regression. **Classification:** substantive.

A portfolio does not normally mirror an index exactly. Its sensitivity to market movements can be represented by beta:

$$r_P-r_f=\alpha+\beta_P(r_M-r_f)+\varepsilon_P.$$

**Notation clarification:** $r_P$ denotes portfolio return, $r_M$ market return, $r_f$ the risk-free return, and $\alpha$ the intercept. The complete **market excess return** $(r_M-r_f)$ is multiplied by $\beta_P$; $\varepsilon_P$ is added outside that product.

## Beta and residual

**Source region:** central beta and residual definitions. **Classification:** substantive.

- $\beta_P$: exposure to systematic market risk.
- $\varepsilon_P$: idiosyncratic return not explained by the market.
- $\beta_P=1$: the portfolio tends to move with the market.
- $\beta_P>1$: greater sensitivity to market movements.

## Beta limits

**Source region:** lower limitation box. **Classification:** substantive.

Beta is estimated from historical data and may change. Index futures hedge systematic risk, **not** the portfolio's idiosyncratic risk.
