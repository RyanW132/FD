## Beta example inputs

**Source region:** upper portfolio and contract notional calculation. **Classification:** substantive.

A portfolio is worth **$5 million** and has $\beta_P=1.20$. The E-mini S&P 500 futures price is **5,000**, so

$$V_F=\$50(5{,}000)=\$250{,}000.$$

$V_F$ is one contract's notional value, not its margin.

## Beta example calculation

**Source region:** middle zero-beta hedge calculation. **Classification:** substantive.

To reduce portfolio beta to zero:

$$N^*=(1.20-0)\left(\frac{5{,}000{,}000}{250{,}000}\right)=24.$$
