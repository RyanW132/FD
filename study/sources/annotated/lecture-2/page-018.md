## Optimal contract number

**Source region:** upper quantity definitions and boxed ratio. **Classification:** substantive.

If $Q_A$ is the size of the exposure and $Q_F$ the quantity covered by **one** futures contract,

$$N^*=h^*\frac{Q_A}{Q_F}.$$

The entire exposure quantity $Q_A$ is divided by per-contract quantity $Q_F$, then multiplied by hedge ratio $h^*$.

## Hedged quantity

**Source region:** middle equivalent-quantity equation. **Classification:** substantive.

The futures position covers the equivalent of

$$N^*Q_F=h^*Q_A$$

units of the exposure.

## Minimum variance limits

**Source region:** lower important box. **Classification:** substantive.

This is a minimum-variance hedge, not necessarily a perfect hedge. Contract rounding and basis risk leave some residual exposure.
