## Hedge ratio summary

**Source region:** central summary, fourth bullet. **Classification:** substantive.

The minimum-variance hedge ratio is

$$h^*=\rho\sigma_S/\sigma_F.$$

$\sigma_S$ and $\sigma_F$ are the standard deviations of spot/futures price changes and $\rho$ their correlation, as defined on physical page 17.

## Whole position principle

**Source region:** bottom core-principle box. **Classification:** substantive.

A hedge should be evaluated by how it changes the risk of the **complete position**, not by whether the derivative makes a profit by itself.
