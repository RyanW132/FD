## Protective put value

**Source region:** upper maturity-value formula. **Classification:** substantive.

At maturity, the value of the hedged position is

$$V_T=1{,}000S_T+1{,}000\max(27.50-S_T,0).$$

$S_T$ is the maturity share price in dollars per share; $V_T$ is the total dollar value of the 1,000 shares plus put payoff. This is terminal **value**, not profit net of the initial portfolio purchase and premium.

## Protective put floor

**Source region:** lower interpretation box. **Classification:** substantive.

The put establishes a minimum terminal value of **$27,500**. Protection is not free: the investor paid a **$1,000 premium initially**.
