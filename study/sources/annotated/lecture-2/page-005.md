## Share profit

**Source region:** middle profit-from-shares box. **Classification:** substantive.

Let $S_T$ be the share price after two months. Profit from 100 shares:

$$\Pi_T^{\mathrm{shares}}=100(S_T-20).$$

The 100 is a share quantity, $S_T$ and 20 are dollars per share, and the resulting profit is dollars.

## Call profit

**Source region:** middle profit-from-calls box. **Classification:** substantive.

Profit from calls covering 2,000 shares:

$$\Pi_T^{\mathrm{calls}}=2{,}000\left[\max(S_T-22.50,0)-1\right].$$

The per-share premium 1 is subtracted **outside** the maximum, inside the bracket multiplied by 2,000. $S_T$ is the share price after two months; 22.50 is the strike, in dollars per share. This is the source's arithmetic-profit convention; no financing adjustment is printed.
