## Stock index definition

**Source region:** upper definition and weighting-rule list. **Classification:** substantive.

A stock index measures the performance of a specified portfolio of shares. Different indices use different weighting rules:
- **S&P 500:** float-adjusted market-capitalisation weighted.
- **Nasdaq-100:** modified market-capitalisation weighted.
- **Dow Jones Industrial Average:** price weighted.

These are statements in the supplied lecture, not independently updated index-methodology documentation.

## Index contract multipliers

**Source region:** middle CME futures examples box. **Classification:** substantive.

CME S&P 500 futures examples:
- **E-mini S&P 500:** $50 × the index.
- **Micro E-mini S&P 500:** $5 × the index.

The multipliers are dollars per index point in these examples; they describe contract notional, not the margin deposit.
