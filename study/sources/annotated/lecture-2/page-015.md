## Contract choice

**Source region:** upper four-step list. **Classification:** substantive.

1. Choose an underlying whose price is closely related to the exposure.
2. Choose a delivery month close to, but normally **later than**, the end of the hedge.
3. Check contract size and calculate the required number of contracts.
4. Consider liquidity, bid–ask spreads, and basis risk.

## Cross hedge definition

**Source region:** lower cross-hedge box. **Classification:** substantive.

When no futures contract exists on the asset being hedged, use a futures contract on a related asset whose price changes are strongly correlated with the exposure.
