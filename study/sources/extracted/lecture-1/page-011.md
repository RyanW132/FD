                             Derivatives: what, why, and how?



 What Do These Contracts Have in Common?


  Consider three contracts
      A contract fixing the price of oil delivered in three months
          A contract paying £100 if the FTSE 100 exceeds a specified level
          A contract paying a farmer if rainfall falls below a threshold

  Question: What determines the value of each contract?
  Key idea
  A derivative is a financial contract whose value depends on the value or
  outcome of an underlying asset, rate, index, or event.

  Examples include forwards, futures, swaps, and options.
