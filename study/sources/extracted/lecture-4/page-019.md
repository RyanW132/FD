                            Valuing Existing Forward Contracts



 Forward Price Is Not Forward Value


  At inception, a fairly priced forward has

                                       K = F0,T                  and           f0 = 0.

  At a later time t:
          the contractual delivery price K remains fixed;
          the market forward price becomes Ft,T ; and
          the contract value ft is generally no longer zero.
  For a long forward,
                                            ft = (Ft,T − K )e −r (T −t) .
  For a short forward, the value is −ft .
