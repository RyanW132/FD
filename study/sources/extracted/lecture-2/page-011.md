                                                Hedging



 Short Hedge for the Sale of an Asset

  A hedger will sell an asset at t2 and shorts futures at t1 . Let F1 be the
  initial futures price, and let S2 and F2 be the spot and futures prices when
  the hedge is closed.
  Effective selling price
  The asset is sold for S2 , and the short futures position earns F1 − F2 .
  Therefore,

                          S2 + (F1 − F2 ) = F1 + b2 ,                      b2 = S2 − F2 .

  Interpretation
  If b2 were known when the hedge began, the effective selling price would
  be known. Uncertainty about b2 creates basis risk.
