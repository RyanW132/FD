                                     Hedging Equity Portfolios



 Why Use a Beta Hedge?


  An investor may have a view about particular shares but not about the
  overall market.
  Example
  An investor expects a portfolio of biopharmaceutical shares to outperform
  the market but is concerned that the entire market may fall. Short index
  futures can reduce the broad market exposure while retaining the
  portfolio’s relative performance exposure.

  What remains after the hedge?
  The hedge reduces estimated systematic risk. Sector-specific,
  company-specific, basis, model, and estimation risks remain.
